# Dev log: 2026-11-25c, building-only comparison, evidence grid, backtest

Branch `headless/2026-11-25c`, built on `headless/2026-11-25b` (PR #1), so it should merge after #1. Written by an AI agent (Claude) working unattended. The project owner approved the changes and asked for the backtest to be re-run.

## What changed

- **The compared number is now building AV ÷ building sq ft.** `build.sql` and `ingest.Columns` (so live mode and the fixture too) load the assessed values' building and land split for every stage (`mailed_*`, `certified_*`, `board_*`). The split comes from the same stage as the total (certified if certified, else mailed). Total AV stays in the data and is shown everywhere.
  - Every surface says why, in the same words: "We compare the building part of your assessment, per square foot, because the Assessor values land separately at a neighborhood rate."
  - **Uniformity figure** = comps' median building rate × the subject's building sq ft **+ the subject's own land AV**, compared with the current total.
  - **Land check:** the subject's land AV per land sq ft against the comps' median, unrounded. More than 1% apart → a "Please verify your land assessment" item that states the figures. It fires for 2.1% of sampled homes.
  - **Missing split:** a record with no split, a $0 building, or a split that doesn't add up to the total (within $1) is excluded from comps, and as a subject it is unsupported, with a reason. In the data: 1 of 406,838 backtest appeals has no split, and 0 of 929,070 2026 records fail to sum.
- **Distance.** Haversine miles between parcel centers. Latitude and longitude were already in the build and in live mode's parcel-universe request, so nothing was added there. Distance is shown in every grid and is part of the similarity score: 1 per mile, capped at 1 mile, with an unknown location counting as 1 mile. Among equally similar homes the nearer comes first. The hard rules (same neighborhood, same class group) are unchanged.
- **Evidence grid** for the subject and every comp: address, PIN, distance, class, stories, construction, building sq ft, year built, beds and baths, basement and finish, central air, fireplaces, garage, land sq ft, building AV, land AV, total AV and building AV per sq ft.
  - **Web:** a list of the evidence comps with their differences, then the full grid. On phones the grid scrolls sideways, with the address column sticky. Tailwind classes only; CSS regenerated.
  - **CLI:** the evidence table gains distance and building, land and total AV, plus a numbered differences list. JSON carries every field.
  - **PDF:** page 2 is now landscape. Only the address may be truncated; any other value is drawn smaller rather than cut, so a figure can't turn into a different number. Tests cover every fixture home, every published characteristic value in bold, values beyond the county's maxima, and a worst-case page.
- **Plain words.** The characteristics dataset (`x54s-btds`) publishes words, not numeric codes (value lists in `docs/data-inventory.md`); `comps/describe.go` rewords them ("Formal Rec Room" → "finished", "0 cars" → "none"). A bare number is hidden, never shown. A fireplace count not on record shows as "–" and is never compared.
- **"Differences from your home"**, one factual line per evidence comp, comp's value first: "0.2 mi away · unfinished basement vs finished · no central air vs central air · no fireplace vs 1 · 1-car garage vs 3-car". It covers distance, class, stories, construction, size (5% or more), age (10 years or more), full and half baths, basement type and finish, central air, fireplaces and garage. The thresholds are below where the PTAB decisions (below) discounted comps, so anything the Board might weigh is listed.
- **Backtest sentences** recomputed (89% / 84% / 64% / 36%) on the site, in reports and docs (the PDF quotes no backtest rate); thresholds kept at 5% and 1%.
- **Task 6 fixes:** search cuts the query at a rune boundary (`truncate`, tested); live mode refuses a neighborhood code that isn't exactly 5 digits (tested, including a quote); the busy limiter's wait is a field (default 5 s) and its 503 path is tested without sleeping. `EstTaxChange` and its test are kept.
- **Backtest command:** adds outcomes by the report's own cut points, the AUC, and `-tsv` (one line per appeal). This was added and run on the old method before anything else changed, so baseline and new are measured the same way. (It was a separate commit at first, but that commit failed lint, so it was folded into the next one, so that every pushed commit passes `scripts/check.sh`.)

## Backtest: baseline vs new

Full details in [backtest-2026-11-25c/README.md](backtest-2026-11-25c/README.md); raw outputs in `baseline/` and `new/`.

| Pro se win rate | Baseline (total AV/sq ft) | New (building AV/sq ft + distance) |
|---|--:|--:|
| ≥ +5% above median | 81.9% (n 24,758) | **88.9%** (n 25,509) |
| +1% to +5% | 77.6% (23,156) | **83.9%** (21,186) |
| within ±1% | 61.6% (21,380) | **63.6%** (18,455) |
| more than 1% below | 39.5% (56,482) | **35.5%** (60,626) |
| **AUC, pro se / attorney / all** | 0.731 / 0.687 / 0.699 | **0.792 / 0.749 / 0.761** |

| Distance weight (building metric) | 0 | 0.5 | **1 (chosen)** | 2 |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Pro se AUC | 0.769 | 0.784 | **0.792** | 0.799 |

- **Tier changes:** 142,283 of 404,578 appeals (35.2%) change tier. The metric alone moves 30.0%. Homes that drop from ≥+5% to below the median have 35.9% of their AV in land, against 22.1% for homes that stay; their mean lot is 17,973 sq ft against 8,418. Example: 01022070100000 (2024) goes from +11.9% to −18.6% with half its AV in land.
- **Evidence report offered:** 39.7% → 38.9% of sampled homes; 36.0% → 34.0% of backtest appeals.
- **Task example** (PIN 13124210120000): $38.01 building-only (was $48.24 total ÷ building sq ft). Comps $29.40–$35.72, all within 0.3 mile. No land flag ($3.55, the neighborhood rate).

### Decision gate

**Passed.** The building-only metric separates winners from losers better (pro se AUC +0.038 at distance weight 0), and so does every tier, so the metric was switched. Distance adds more (+0.023 at weight 1). I chose weight 1 over 2 to keep similarity ahead of nearness (reasoning in the README).

## What PTAB decisions showed

I read 20 Cook County residential equity decisions (dockets 24-20180 to 24-20214, issued in 2026). The fetches went to ptab.illinois.gov only, about 45 requests (dockets 24-20180 to 24-20224, most of which don't exist), 0.3 s apart. A small scratch extractor ran outside the repo's tracked files; 4 agents then read 5 decisions each. Party and attorney names appear in the decisions and are recorded nowhere.

- All 20 were represented, all argued improvement inequity, and **none contested land**: the Board compares improvement assessment per square foot of living area, as the task said. 2 got a reduction and 18 no change.
- Characteristics the Board cited for giving comps less weight, or for calling them "most similar" (decisions of 20; broader counts include "similar in …" lists):
  - dwelling size: 15 (18);
  - age: 12 (15);
  - location or proximity: 10 (12);
  - design or stories: 8;
  - garage: 7;
  - central air: 3 (6);
  - basement or finish: 3 (6);
  - same neighborhood code or block: 3;
  - bathrooms: 2 (plus several adjustments);
  - construction: 2;
  - class: 2;
  - fireplaces: 0 (1 adjustment);
  - site size: 0.
- **Size:** comps 6–19% smaller were discounted when closer matches existed. One 17% gap was accepted with an adjustment, and 22% was tolerated to stay in the neighborhood.
- **Age:** a 24–26 year gap was the one explicit cutoff. For very old homes, wider gaps were tolerated.
- **Distance:** comps on the same block or within ¼ mile were preferred; 0.6–1.3 miles, or a different neighborhood, got less weight.
- Most decisions dropped some comps without giving a reason, so these are tendencies, not rules. Our thresholds (5%, 10 years, any distance) sit below them on purpose.

## Final review

An independent agent reviewed the branch and found 11 issues, all fixed except where noted:

- **Fixed:**
  - The web headline put the building-only gap on "your home". It now says the building, per square foot.
  - Headline and reasons used different cut points for "in line" (±0.5% vs ±1%); this was there before this branch. There is now one shared `comps.InLine`.
  - The PDF misdescribed page 2 and left distance out of the selection text.
  - A missing fireplace count was shown as 0.
  - The land check compared rounded rates, which produced false flags: 79 became 42 in the sample.
  - There was no check that building + land = total.
  - Page 2 could overflow on a worst-case report (a test now covers it).
  - The proximity test didn't actually exercise the tie-break.
  - A no-address comp in the PDF's differences list had no name.
  - Stale "AV per sq ft" comments.
  - Duplicate below-count code.
- **Not changed:** cut points are still written separately in `eval.tierBucket`. It is measurement code, and it must keep measuring the same way across runs.

## Open questions

1. **Distance weight:** 1 (chosen) or 2 (+0.007 AUC)? A higher weight favors the nearest homes over the most alike.
2. **Tiers:** 1–5% now wins 84% against 89% at ≥5%. Is the "possible" tier still a useful distinction, or should the PDF threshold be revisited?
3. **JSON for unknown fireplaces** is `-1`. Would `null` be better? It would make the field a pointer.
4. **Homes that jump into the top tier** (e.g. 29042020210000: −18.6% → +137.6%) are worth a manual look. Their comps changed a lot, partly because of distance.
5. **The land flag** fires for 2.1% of homes. Is 1% the right tolerance, or should it say more about why a land rate might differ (for example, an irregular lot)?
