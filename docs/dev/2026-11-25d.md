## What was built

### A. Township pages

- **`GET /townships`** lists all 38 townships in four groups: open now (with days left), opening soon, dates not posted yet, and closed for this session. Status is computed on every request from `bor.StatusOf`; nothing is hardcoded. Each row links to its township page and shows its dates.
- **`GET /townships/{slug}`** with stable slugs (`lake-view`, `new-trier`, `hyde-park`, `north-chicago`).
  - Variants redirect permanently (301) to the canonical slug: `lakeview`, `Lake_View`, `new-trier-township`, `Hyde%20Park`. `/townships/` redirects to `/townships`. Unknown names get the site's 404. `lake` and `lake-view` are different townships and both resolve correctly.
  - **Board of Review window:** status, open, close and evidence dates, days left, the Board's group number and when the schedule was checked. When dates aren't posted: "The Board hasn't posted dates yet (as of November 24, 2026)". Links: "File on the Board's portal" while open, "Pre-register with the Board" while upcoming or not posted (the facts doc supports pre-registering only before a township opens, so closed townships don't get it), and the Board's dates page always.
  - **The address/PIN search box** (the existing `SearchBox`).
  - **Facts from the data**, loaded once at startup (`loadTownshipFacts`), like the site's other numbers:
    - homes in the classes AssessGap checks (202–210, 234, 278, 295, from `comps.ScopeClasses`), by `parcel.township_name`;
    - Board of Review outcomes for self-filed and attorney/firm appeals: appeals, share reduced and average reduction, for the last three tax years (2023–2025 in today's data). These aggregate `nbhd_bor_stats`, the neighborhood table the reports use, to the township. A neighborhood belongs to the township whose Assessor township code is its first two digits; the `99999` catch-all neighborhood belongs to none. Counts are summed, and the average reduction is the neighborhood averages weighted by their reduction counts, which equals the average over all reductions. The comparison metric isn't used.
    - The source line names the data, the year range and the filter (residential appeals other than condominiums), and repeats the report's caveat that these are outcomes for people who chose to appeal.
  - **How to file on your own:** five steps from `docs/bor-filing-facts.md` and `internal/bor` constants: PIN, the portal and "Property Over-Assessed", the evidence deadline and the brief's photo (Rule 14(A)), hearings by phone, decisions in 8–16 weeks, re-review in 3 days and PTAB in 30 days. Plus Board Rule 1, quoted, and links to How it works and the filing notes.
  - **The township email signup** (`NotifyFormFor` with reason `township-opens`, carrying the slug). It's hidden while the township is open. The handler validates the slug and stores the canonical township name in the existing `signup.township` column, so no schema change was needed. A PIN's own township still wins when both are present.
- **Links in:** a "Find your township" card on the home page, "Appeal dates by township" in the footer, and "<Township> Township dates and steps" in every report's filing-window banner.
- **SEO:** a unique title and meta description per page, a canonical link on every indexed page (so `/?utm_source=x` names `/`), `/townships` and all 38 township pages in `sitemap.xml`, and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD on township pages (no FAQ markup). Everything is server-rendered; no JavaScript is needed.
- **Signup without JavaScript:** posting the email form without htmx used to return a bare fragment. It now returns the result in the site layout. The result is announced to screen readers (`role="status"` and `role="alert"`), and the input is marked `aria-invalid` on error.

### B. Link previews

- Every page carries Open Graph and Twitter card tags: title, description, URL, image (1200×630, with alt text). They come from a `views.Share` value that `render` puts in the request context, so `Layout`'s signature and every page template are unchanged.
- **Report pages get a generic title, description and `og:url` (the home page).** No address or PIN appears in any preview tag. `TestSharePreview` checks this for both PIN spellings and the address, and fails if the report path stops being treated as private (checked by mutation). The page's `<title>` keeps the address.
- **`internal/web/static/og.png`** (37 KB): the "AG" badge, "AssessGap", "Free, open-source Cook County property tax appeal check" and assessgap.com, in the site's emerald and slate, drawn by `tools/ogimage` with the Go fonts. Two runs give the same file. It adds `golang.org/x/image` (BSD-3, the Go project's own fonts and rasteriser), used only by the tool. The site binary doesn't link it.

## Open questions

1. **Several townships per email.** `signup` is unique on `(email, pin)`, and township-page signups have no PIN, so a second township with the same email is silently kept as the first. Fixing it needs a new unique key: a new table, or a migration of `signup`. Worth deciding before township emails are built.
2. **"Filing at the Board of Review is free"** appears across the site, but `docs/bor-filing-facts.md` doesn't cite a source for it. The township pages leave it out until there's a cited source. The share tagline "Free, open-source Cook County property tax appeal check" is used as specified. The compliance reviewer noted that "appeal check" could be read as "check whether to appeal"; reword it if you agree, and rerun `tools/ogimage`.
3. **The home page FAQ** says the remaining townships open "between December and February". The facts doc marks that as not confirmed. It's outside this branch's scope, so I left it.
4. **Signup stores the township name, not the slug.** The name is in the column's existing convention, and the slug is `strings.ReplaceAll(strings.ToLower(name), " ", "-")`. Say if you'd rather have a separate slug column.
5. **Homes count:** it counts parcels by class, and the page says so: "N parcels in classes 202–210, 234, 278 and 295 … A few, such as parcels with more than one building, can't be checked yet". An exact count of checkable homes would mean reusing the engine's support rules, which the other branch is changing.
6. **Fixture privacy** (raised again by the compliance reviewer, from PR #1): the fixture has public-record addresses and sale prices tied to PINs, but no owner names. This branch doesn't change it.
