# UX audit: navigation and usability, 2026-11-26

Branch `headless/2026-11-26-ux`. Written by an AI agent (Claude) working unattended. It audits `main` at `3e2e2f1`, before any change.

## How this was done

- **Served:** `main`, on 127.0.0.1, against a read-only copy of the full county database.
- **Screenshots: none.** The session's permissions blocked launching Chrome, so `scripts/screenshots.sh` couldn't run. The audit used two things instead:
  - the rendered HTML of `/`, `/townships`, `/townships/hyde-park`, `/check/13124210120000`, `/how-it-works` and both 404s;
  - the templates, reading layout from the Tailwind classes. The `sm:` breakpoint is 640 px, so unprefixed classes are what a 390 px phone gets.
- **Reviewers:** five independent ones (navigation and wayfinding, mobile, accessibility, content length, cross-page consistency). Each returned findings with a severity.
- **Verification:** I checked every finding kept below against the markup or the generated `_templ.go`. Pixel heights are estimates from the classes, not measurements.

The four journeys:
1. Address, then report, then PDF, then how to file.
2. A visitor from Google lands on a township page.
3. A shared report link opened cold on a phone.
4. A returning visitor wants the dates or How it works.

## Findings, by priority

### P0: navigation (the maintainer's complaint)

1. **The header has no way to the township dates, and phones get one link.**
   - The nav is `How it works` (`hidden sm:inline`) plus `Check my home` (`layout.templ:27-30`).
   - `/townships` is linked only from the footer and from a card below the stats on the home page. On a phone that card comes after about three screens of hero and stacked stat cards.
   - Nothing marks the current page.
   - "Check my home" (`/#check`) does nothing on the home page itself.
   - Journeys: all four.
2. **`/townships` assumes you know your township, and lists by status only.**
   - The 38 townships are in three status groups (open, not posted, closed), each in schedule order. Finding one name means scanning all three, about four phone screens.
   - Nothing tells you how to find your township from your address.
   - The search results don't show the township either: `views.Match` has address, city, kind and PIN only. Within Chicago, the city doesn't tell eight townships apart.
3. **Township pages are dead ends sideways.**
   - They have no links to other townships, only a small grey breadcrumb.
   - An unknown township (`/townships/nowhere`) gets the generic "We couldn't find that page or PIN" page, with no link to `/townships`.
4. **The report gives no route to "how to file".**
   - "File yourself, for free" (`report.templ:265`) has no link.
   - The township page's filing steps are reachable only from the window banner at the top, and only when a banner is shown. `server.go:158` sets the township link only when `Window.Text != ""`.
   - The PDF button comes after the 31-row, 18-column table: around screen 7 of about 10 on a phone.
5. **A shared report opened cold explains nothing.** It starts with "← Check another home", an address and a status banner. On a phone, "How it works" is only in the footer, below about 6,800 px of page.

### P0: known bugs (PR #3 review)

6. **The sticky grid cells paint over the site header.** Both are `z-10` (`layout.templ:21`, `report.templ:176,220`), and the cells come later in the DOM.
7. **"$29.40per sq ft".** templ drops the leading space inside `<span> per sq ft</span>` (`report.templ:161` → `report_templ.go:1296`). It shows on all 6 evidence rows. No other on-screen drop was found, comparing every text/element boundary in the generated files.
   - Two places join words only when copied or read aloud: "Your home14-…" (the PIN `block` span) and "2026Mailed value".
8. **The grid caption and the chart legend overstate the shading.**
   - Only the up-to-6 evidence homes are green (`rowBG`; `comps.maxEvidence = 6`).
   - The caption says "Highlighted rows are similar homes whose building is assessed lower…", and the legend's grey "Other similar homes" includes lower-assessed homes.
   - For 13124210120000, all 20 are lower and 6 are green.
9. **The quoted back-test rate has no caveat beside it.**
   - "…got a reduction about 92% of the time" is a summary bullet from `comps.decide` (`analyze.go:226`). The only caveat on the page is three sections further down.
   - The home page's "61% of homeowners who appealed on their own… got a reduction" stat has none.
   - The CLI text and the PDF don't quote the back-test rate. The CLI's neighborhood rates already carry the caveat on the next line. The PDF quotes no rate at all.

### P1: mobile and accessibility

10. **Tap targets under 44 px.**
    - Header CTA: about 32 px.
    - Logo link: 28 px.
    - Footer links: 20 px tall with 4 px between rows.
    - Breadcrumbs and back link: 20 px.
    - Window and portal links: 28 px.
11. **Contrast (WCAG 1.4.3).**
    - White on `emerald-600` is 3.66:1 (the Check button, home CTA, strong badge). White on `emerald-500` is 2.47:1 (PDF and Notify buttons). White on `amber-500` is 2.13:1 (the "possible" badge).
    - `slate-400` text is about 2.6:1 on white and stone-50 (PINs, disclaimer, stat footnotes, search result kind/PIN, chart ticks).
    - The notify form's `rose-400` error and `opacity-60` note fail on the white "unsupported" card (2.9:1 and 4.0:1), though they pass on the dark cards.
12. **Focus and announcements.**
    - Search result links have `outline-none` with only a near-white background on focus.
    - `#results` isn't a live region.
    - No skip link.
    - The sticky header can cover a focused element (no `scroll-padding-top`).
    - The scrollable grid can't take keyboard focus.
    - Grid headers have no `scope`.
13. **The home page buries the search on phones.** Hero `pt-20`, a 4–5 line `text-4xl` h1 and a 6-line intro put the input around 620–700 px down.

### P2: content and consistency

14. **The report repeats itself.**
    - The summary bullets restate the headline, the badge and the number cards.
    - The "building part" sentence appears twice within one screen.
    - The bullets come from `comps.Report.Reasons`, which the CLI JSON also uses.
15. **Report section order.** The 31-row table sits between the evidence and everything a homeowner acts on (flags, options, PDF).
16. **How it works.**
    - The back-test paragraph is under "If an appeal doesn't work".
    - The FAQ lives on the home page, and its "When can I appeal?" answer hardcodes "between December and February".
17. **Township page order.** The filing steps come after the outcomes table.
18. **Style drift.**
    - Three link styles for the same Board link.
    - Two button greens.
    - `slate` vs `stone` badges.
    - The error page has no links, and its email address isn't a link.

## What this branch changes

- **1–5:**
  - A site header on every page (Check my home, Townships, How it works) with `aria-current`. On phones it's a no-JS `<details>` menu.
  - Breadcrumbs on the townships index, township pages and reports. The BreadcrumbList data now uses the same names as the visible crumbs.
  - `/townships`: an A–Z list of all 38 townships and a "Find your township" address search, whose results name the township and link to its page.
  - Township pages: previous/next township links and "All townships"; the filing steps get an anchor.
  - The report: an "On this page" menu with the PDF button; options and flags moved above the big grid; "How to file in X Township" links; a one-line intro for cold visitors; the township is always linked.
  - Footer grouped, with 44 px targets.
  - A township 404 and a generic 404 with links onward.
- **6–9:**
  - The four bugs above. For item 9, the decided sentence follows the back-test rate in `comps` (web report and JSON).
  - The neighborhood and township caveats use the same "any one home" wording.
  - The home stat gets a caveat.
  - A compliance test now requires the caveat near every quoted reduction rate, on every scanned surface: JSON, PDF, CLI text and website.
- **10–13:** 44 px targets, the contrast fixes, focus outlines, a live region, a skip link, scroll padding, a focusable grid, and `scope`. The home hero is tighter on phones.
- **16–17:**
  - The FAQ moves to How it works, inside `<details>`, and the dated months go.
  - The back-test paragraph gets its own heading.
  - Township pages show the filing steps before the outcomes.

## Left as proposals (for the maintainer)

- **Trim the report's summary bullets (14).**
  - Showing only the first bullet plus the widened-search and small-group notes on the web would remove most of the repetition.
  - The bullets come from `internal/comps`, which this run doesn't own, and the CLI JSON shares them. So it's either a filter in `web.toView` (the web report and the JSON would then differ) or a change in `comps`. That needs a decision.
- **Collapse the full grid behind `<details>`**, or show 8 rows and "Show all". It would cut about 1,800 px on a phone. The grid is the evidence itself, so hiding it is a judgment call.
- **Move the window banner below the summary on phones.** The headline would then start on the first screen. But the banner carries the deadline, which some visitors came for.
- **Township neighbors by geography** rather than alphabetical previous/next. This needs adjacency data that isn't in the database.
- **Chart dots:** the emerald-500 and slate-300 dots are under 3:1 against white (1.4.11). Darker fills, or a different shape for "other" homes, would fix it.
- **Truncate the grid's sticky address column on phones.** It takes about half the 350 px width.
